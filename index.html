Hawk

Hawk, a browser that stays out of your way. Small, written in C, no telemetry, no account, no "AI", no drama. Search runs on your machine or not at all.

curl -fsSL https://sf0e.github.io/hawk-site/get/install.sh | sh or build it from source · gone of it? uninstall

what it is

A GTK4 + WebKitGTK browser written in C. One pill-shaped bar up top, small pill tabs below it, and nothing else. Anything that tries to get between you and the page loses. the whole list of ways it stays out of your way →

  • remembers the URLs you've been to. types ahead. like any browser should
  • cookies persist in ~/.hawk/cookies — your logins survive — nothing is wiped on exit
  • your last session comes back when you reopen it
  • third-party cookies blocked, camera & mic off, one fixed user agent
  • dark mode that also turns the web dark. yes, for real

the search part

Hawk keeps a private SearXNG on 127.0.0.1:8080. It boots when you open the browser, dies when you close it, and your queries never leave the machine. If the backend can't start, Hawk sighs and uses DuckDuckGo instead. how it works →

status

alpha. v0.1.0-alpha is out and the install command at the top of this page works. Linux only. It's a working browser — tabs, pills, dark mode, local search, URL memory, sessions — with a few honest rough edges, listed in the README, which I recommend reading out loud to the browser.
